Dephasing, not loss

Gain holds the energy, but the long-lived modes are global Laguerre–Gauss modes whose phases drift apart by ~5×10⁻⁴ rad per order each trip. A dot’s shape dissolves and then revives (corr 0.99 at 2.3×10⁴ trips).

Vignetting sets the field

The 1.2 mm relay lenses cut per-cell retention to 0.09 in the corners of a ±400 µm lattice after one trip. Only ±200 µm (0.16 mm², 20×20 SLM pixels) behaves; with it excluded, every encoding decodes the same.

No permanent linear state

Every encoding at every pitch fails by 2–4×10³ trips. Mask disorder (the preset random SLM program) cuts lattice lifetimes 8× and dot lifetimes up to 100×; a 1 % relay focus error cuts dot lifetimes 40×.
