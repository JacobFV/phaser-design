Modes are global, dots are superpositions

The longest-lived eigenmodes of the self-imaging ring are centred Laguerre–Gauss patterns, degenerate in magnitude to 10⁻¹¹. 85 of them share the same 0.3 mm² — many concurrent latent components, but as modes, not pixels.

Étendue is the bottleneck

Mode counts match the relay étendue (field ≈ 0.3 mm radius × NA ≈ 5 mrad), not the 4096 SLM pixels. Coupling stays nearest-neighbour to ~10³ trips in A (115 µm radius at 1024) and ≥ 128 in B, then becomes long-range as light dephases.
